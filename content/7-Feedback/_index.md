## Sharing and Feedback

### Overall Evaluation

****1. Working Environment****

The working environment in the First Cloud AI Journey program was friendly and open. Throughout the program, I felt comfortable discussing the problems I encountered during the learning and project development process. The mentors and other members were approachable and willing to provide support, which made it easier for me to ask questions and become familiar with new knowledge and technologies.

In particular, being exposed to a technology- and cloud-oriented environment gave me more motivation to explore the field I am pursuing. This was also one of the aspects that made my internship experience meaningful.

****2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin****

I greatly appreciated the support from my mentor throughout the program. Whenever I encountered something I did not understand or did not know how to handle, I could discuss it and receive clear guidance and explanations. What I particularly appreciated was that I was often encouraged to research and try to solve problems on my own before receiving specific guidance.

This approach helped me not only complete my tasks but also develop my ability to think independently, search for information, and patiently troubleshoot problems. I believe these are valuable skills that will continue to support me in my future studies and career.

In addition, the team admin provided support for program-related matters and provided the necessary information and materials, allowing me to focus more effectively on learning and project development.

****3. Relevance of Work to My Academic Major****

The content and tasks in the program were highly relevant to my major in Information Technology. Knowledge of programming, databases, frontend, backend, and systems that I had learned at university could be applied during the project.

At the same time, the program allowed me to gain deeper exposure to cloud computing and AWS services, areas in which I had not had many opportunities to practice before. Being able to directly deploy and integrate different system components helped me understand how concepts that I had previously learned separately could be combined to build a complete application.

****4. Learning and Skill Development Opportunities****

During the program, I had the opportunity to learn and practice many new concepts related to AWS and cloud computing. Directly deploying application components helped me better understand how a system operates in a real-world environment rather than only learning through theory.

In addition to technical knowledge, I also improved my self-learning, troubleshooting, task management, and technical communication skills. The project also helped me realize that when working with technology, being able to independently research and solve problems is just as important as knowing how to use a particular tool or service.

These experiences helped me better understand the skills that I need to continue developing if I want to pursue a career in Cloud and Information Technology.

****5. Company Culture and Team Spirit****

I found that the program had a supportive and collaborative environment. When I encountered difficulties during the project, I could communicate with other members to discuss possible solutions. Working and exchanging ideas with people who had different levels of experience also gave me additional perspectives when approaching technical problems.

I particularly appreciated the supportive spirit within the program. As an intern who was still learning, being able to ask questions and receive guidance helped me feel much more confident when approaching unfamiliar technologies and concepts.

****6. Internship Policies and Benefits****

The program provided the necessary conditions for interns to learn and practice effectively. Having access to AWS learning materials, guidance, and a practical environment was very valuable to me.

In addition, participating in a real-world program related to cloud computing and having the opportunity to work directly with AWS technologies was a valuable experience for me as an Information Technology student. It also helped me gain a clearer perspective on potential career paths in the technology field.

---

### Additional Questions

****1. What did you find most satisfying during your internship?****

The aspect I found most satisfying was having the opportunity to work directly with AWS and apply what I had learned to a real project. Instead of only studying individual services separately, I was able to see how the frontend, backend, database, and cloud services could work together to form a complete system.

In particular, building and deploying KnoVerse helped me better understand the challenges that can arise when moving an application from a local environment to production. There were situations where I initially did not know how to solve a problem and had to spend time researching, experimenting, and testing different approaches. However, these experiences helped me learn more and become more confident when dealing with similar problems.

This was a valuable experience for me because it showed me that practical knowledge does not come only from studying theory, but also from actually implementing solutions, making mistakes, and finding ways to resolve them.

****2. What do you think the company/program should improve for future interns?****

I think the program could provide a clearer learning roadmap or checklist for each stage so that interns can track their progress more easily and identify which areas they should prioritize. This would be especially helpful for interns who are new to AWS and may not know where to begin.

In addition, I would appreciate having more technical sharing sessions or practical workshops. Currently, the number of sharing sessions and the duration of each session are relatively limited, so there are times when I and other interns do not have enough time to ask questions or discuss the issues we are facing in greater depth.

If more time could be allocated to Q&A sessions or additional in-depth sharing sessions could be organized on topics such as troubleshooting, system design, security, or cost optimization, I believe the learning experience for interns could be further improved.

****3. If recommending to a friend, would you suggest they intern here? Why or why not?****

Yes. I would recommend the program to friends who are interested in cloud computing, AWS, or learning more about working in the technology industry.

The program provides interns with opportunities to work with real technologies, gain hands-on experience, and learn from people with practical experience. For me, the greatest value of the program was not only the AWS knowledge I gained but also the experience of learning and solving problems in a practical environment.

I believe the program is particularly suitable for students who are willing to learn independently, take initiative, and are not afraid to explore when facing unfamiliar problems.

---

### Suggestions and Expectations

****1. Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?****

I suggest that the program could increase the number of technical sharing sessions or practical workshops while also allocating more time for discussion and Q&A. Sharing sessions with experienced professionals would help interns better understand how AWS knowledge and technologies are applied in real-world work.

In addition, organizing more social activities between interns or between interns and team members could also be beneficial. These activities would not only help everyone get to know each other better but also create opportunities for interns to share experiences and support one another during the project.

****2. Would you like to continue this program in the future?****

Yes. I would be very interested in participating in similar programs in the future, especially programs that allow me to continue developing my knowledge of AWS, cloud computing, and AI-related technologies.

After completing this internship, I realized that there are still many areas I need to learn more about. Therefore, if I have the opportunity, I would like to work on more complex projects to further improve my system design, deployment, and operation skills, as well as gain a deeper understanding of how cloud-based systems are built and maintained in practice.

****3. Any other comments (free sharing)****

I am very grateful for the opportunity to participate in the Workforce Bootcamp - First Cloud AI Journey program. This was not only a period in which I learned more about AWS but also an opportunity to reflect on the knowledge I had gained at university and understand how it could be applied in a real-world environment.

Throughout the program, there were times when I encountered problems and initially did not know where to start. However, researching information, reading documentation, experimenting, and checking each step gradually helped me find solutions. These experiences taught me more about the value of patience and being proactive when working with technology.

I also feel fortunate to have had the opportunity to learn from my mentor, the team, and other members of the program. Their support and willingness to share their experiences helped me become more confident when stepping outside my familiar academic environment and experiencing real-world work.

Overall, this was a very meaningful experience for my academic development and career orientation. I hope that the knowledge, experience, and lessons I gained from this program will become a strong foundation for my continued development in Cloud Computing and Information Technology.
