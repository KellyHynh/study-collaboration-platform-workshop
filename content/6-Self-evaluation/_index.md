During my internship at Amazon Web Services Viet Nam Company Limited from 01/06/2026 to 01/09/2026, I had the opportunity to learn, practice, and apply the knowledge acquired at university to a real-world working environment. Through the Workforce Bootcamp - First Cloud AI Journey program, I was introduced to various concepts and technologies related to cloud computing, particularly Amazon Web Services (AWS), while also having the opportunity to practice deploying and building an application in the AWS environment.

During the internship, I participated in activities related to learning about AWS, designing and deploying system components, building and developing the KnoVerse application, and practicing the integration between the frontend, backend, and AWS services. Through these activities, I was able to strengthen my knowledge of cloud computing, AWS services, frontend, backend, databases, deployment, system integration, and troubleshooting. I also improved my ability to analyze problems, read technical documentation, and resolve issues that occurred during the deployment process.

Regarding my work ethic, I always tried to complete assigned tasks according to the requirements, proactively seek solutions when encountering problems, and follow the rules and requirements of the program. In addition, I focused on documenting the implementation process, verifying the results after each step, and adjusting my approach when problems occurred. **Areas for Improvement**

In addition to the achievements gained during the internship, I recognize that there are still several areas that I need to improve in order to better meet the requirements of a real-world working environment.

**1. In-depth Knowledge of AWS and Cloud Computing**

Although I had the opportunity to practice with various AWS services, my current knowledge is still mainly focused on understanding and implementing the functions required for the project. I need to continue developing a deeper understanding of cloud architecture, system scalability, availability, security, cost optimization, and appropriate deployment approaches for different types of workloads.

**2. Troubleshooting Skills**

During the project, I was able to handle basic issues by checking configurations, logs, and individual system components. However, when dealing with more complex problems involving multiple services or layers of the system, I still needed considerable time to identify the root cause. Therefore, I need to further develop a systematic troubleshooting approach and improve my ability to analyze logs, monitoring data, and tracing information.

**3. Time Management**

When working on a project with multiple components, I sometimes spent too much time focusing on a particular issue before moving on to other tasks. In the future, I need to improve my ability to prioritize tasks, estimate the time required, and track progress in order to use my time more effectively.

**4. Technical Documentation and Research Skills**

I have developed the habit of proactively searching for and reading documentation when encountering problems. However, when dealing with more advanced technical documentation, I sometimes need considerable time to fully understand the content and identify the information that is most relevant to the issue. I need to improve my ability to read technical documentation systematically, identify appropriate keywords, and quickly apply information from documentation to practical solutions.
